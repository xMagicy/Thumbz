Trailer & commercial content filter (toevoegen aan passesPreClassifierFilters)
Een video wordt direct geweigerd bij ELK van deze signals.

Filter 1: Title trailer keywords
Title bevat (case-insensitive, regex word boundary):

/\b(trailer|teaser|first look|sneak peek|coming soon|in theaters|now streaming|premieres|premiere|official trailer|concept trailer|fan trailer|behind the scenes)\b/i
Filter 2: Year-in-parens + trailer pattern
Title matched ANY van:

/\(\d{4}\).*(trailer|teaser|first look)/i
/(trailer|teaser|first look).*\(\d{4}\)/i
Dekt: "Knight Rider Rebirth (2026) | Concept Trailer", "Concept Trailer (2026)", etc.

Filter 3: Cast list pattern (commercial movies hebben deze)
Title bevat patroon Naam, Naam(, Naam)+ |:

/[A-Z][a-z]+ [A-Z][a-z]+,\s*[A-Z][a-z]+ [A-Z][a-z]+/
Verklaring: "Jensen Ackles, Mads Mikkelsen, Scarlett Johansson" pattern. Bijna altijd een cast announcement = trailer.

Filter 4: Description commercial markers
Description bevat (als beschikbaar):

/\b(in theaters|streaming on|now playing|available now|releases on|premieres on|catch (it|him|her) on|presented by|sponsored by|in association with|all rights reserved)\b/i
Filter 5: Channel name suffix (uitbreiding van bestaande lijst)
Channel name ends with OR contains as whole word:

Studios, Pictures, Films, Productions, Entertainment Group,
Media, TV, Network, Channel (suffix), Official, Records,
Music Group, Animation, Concept, Trailers, Movies
Voorbeelden die nu zouden falen: "Ultimate Studios", "Marvel Entertainment", "Concept Trailers", "Movieclips", "Trailer Park Studios".

Filter 6: CategoryId 1 (Film & Animation)
Al in plaats, behouden. Plus: alle videos van categoryId 24 (Entertainment) waar title een trailer keyword bevat.

Filter 7: Channel subscriber count vs view distribution
Movie/trailer channels hebben vaak honderden miljoenen views op enkele videos en lage subs ratio. Verdacht patroon:

if (subscriber_count < 1M AND single_video_view_count > 10M) → flag for review
Niet auto-skip maar wel logging.

Aggressive cleanup van bestaande data
-- Markeer alle bestaande trailers + commercial content als archived
UPDATE thumbnails 
SET archived = true 
WHERE archived IS NOT true 
  AND (
    title ~* '\b(trailer|teaser|first look|sneak peek|coming soon|in theaters|now streaming|premieres|concept trailer)\b'
    OR title ~* '\(\d{4}\).*\b(trailer|teaser)\b'
    OR channel_name ~* '\b(studios|pictures|films|productions|movieclips|trailers|entertainment group|media group)\b'
    OR channel_name ILIKE 'Ultimate Studios'
  );
